1. Data on your device

The app stores the information needed for the features you use in its private device storage:

  • Favorite recipes, personal collection names and the recipes in those collections.
  • Shopping list ingredients, recipe names and checked items; planned meals and their dates.
  • Cooking progress, checked ingredients, timer deadlines and recently opened recipes.
  • Your recipe notes, personal ratings, cooking history and reduced-motion preference.

These records support your library and cooking tools. The current Android app does not transmit these saved records to us or advertisers. Search terms and ingredient filters are used locally and kept in page memory, not sent to a search service. Your operating system and chosen keyboard have their own privacy practices.

There is no separate external analytics or crash-reporting SDK configured by us in the current Android app. The advertising SDK processes the information described in section 3. Device or app-store diagnostic reporting you enable separately is governed by that provider's settings.

Cooking-step illustration downloads

When you open a supported cooking step whose illustration is not already cached, the app requests the image over HTTPS from worldrecipesathome.com, hosted by SmarterASP.NET. The request exposes your IP address, the requested image address and technical connection information to that website and its hosting provider. The image address can identify the recipe and step you opened. We do not attach your saved notes, shopping list, meal plan, ratings, advertising ID or a signed-in app identity to these image requests.

Downloaded illustrations are checked against the app's image catalog and cached in app-private storage. The cache is limited and older images can be replaced; clearing app storage or uninstalling also removes it. The seven included collections' recipe text and ingredients remain available offline even when an illustration cannot be downloaded. Image requests are separate from advertising requests and are used to deliver and protect the image service, not to synchronize your cooking records.

2. Permissions and timers

On Android, optional notification and alarm permissions support cooking timer alerts. The app may use vibration and restore an active timer after a device restart. If you decline the necessary permissions, background alerts may be unavailable; foreground cooking remains available.

The current app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts or precise device location. Internet and network-state permissions support advertising in advertising-enabled builds and online library services; a permission alone does not mean your saved recipes are uploaded.

3. Advertising and your choices

Advertising-enabled Android builds use Google AdMob. Advertising-disabled builds do not include the advertising SDK. A build configured for live ads uses the app's own ad units on physical devices; test-ad builds and emulators use test ad units. Test ads can still involve network requests and data processing.

In an advertising-enabled build, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may process IP addresses (including approximate location derived from IP), advertising or other applicable device identifiers, ad and app interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. Google uses SDK data for ad delivery and measurement, analytics and fraud prevention, as described in its Mobile Ads data disclosure and Privacy Policy. Your local recipe notes and shopping lists are not provided to the SDK by us.

Banners may appear in library or country browsing areas and after the ingredients on a recipe page. There are no ad placements in step-by-step cooking or timer views. You may choose to watch a rewarded video for 24-hour access to one selected country. An active monthly subscription removes advertising placements while subscription access remains valid. A previous lifetime purchase, where applicable, retains its original access terms.

Advertising-enabled builds use Google's User Messaging Platform to request any consent required for your region before ad requests are allowed. Where required, the app's Settings page provides Ad privacy choices to revisit your decision. A failed consent setup does not block offline cooking. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily prevent all advertising-related data processing. Android also provides advertising-ID controls in device settings. Advertising-disabled builds do not display an ad consent form.

4. Subscriptions, country access and online services

Google Play handles monthly subscription checkout, payment and cancellation. We do not receive your full payment card number. A subscription renews automatically until canceled; Google Play displays the price and billing terms before purchase. You can manage or cancel it in Google Play. The app refreshes subscription access online and uses a time-limited, verified access record on your device between checks.

Online library requests use api.worldrecipesathome.com, hosted by SmarterASP.NET. When the app connects, it sends a randomly generated installation identifier and installation credential. The server stores a credential hash, a service identity and an obfuscated account binding to authenticate later requests. This service identity is not a Google sign-in identity and does not contain a name or email address. The credential is held in protected device storage; it is not itself proof of a paid subscription.

When you purchase, restore or refresh paid access, the app sends the relevant Google Play purchase token and product/access information to our service. The service checks the current purchase with Google Play and processes subscription state, billing-plan information, expiry, acknowledgment and related transaction identifiers. Server entitlement ledgers retain hashed purchase identifiers and access observations to prevent replay and track current rights. Google Play sends subscription-change notifications through Google Cloud Pub/Sub so the service can recheck renewals, cancellation and other supported subscription changes.

For a rewarded country unlock, the service records the selected country, installation/account binding, reward request and its timing. The app supplies a pseudonymous user binding and reward-request identifier to Google AdMob. Google sends a signed reward confirmation containing ad and reward transaction information to our service. The service verifies it, records a transaction hash to prevent duplicate rewards and issues a time-limited access record. A subscription is not required for this option.

Country downloads send an authenticated request identifying the collection you selected. Recipe packs are checked and saved in app-private storage. These service requests expose your IP address and technical connection information to the service and hosting provider; hosting security/access logs may also contain requested paths and connection metadata. Your recipe notes, meal plans, shopping lists and cooking history are not included in purchase verification or country-download requests.

5. Support and disclosure

If you email us, we receive your email address and any name, message or attachments you choose to send. We use these to respond, troubleshoot and handle privacy requests. Please send only information needed for your request, not passwords or payment card details. Email is processed through our email provider, Gmail; see Google's Privacy Policy.

The app's Export my saved data command creates a local file and opens your device's sharing tools. Nothing is automatically emailed to us. The destination you select receives the file under its own terms and privacy practices.

We do not sell your locally saved cooking data. Information you send us may be disclosed where required by law or necessary to establish or defend legal claims. Service providers handle information needed to deliver the services described here; there is no automatic upload of your local library to our own server.

6. Retention and deletion

Your saved library stays on the device until you change it, clear the app's storage through Android settings or uninstall the app. A local recovery copy may exist if a saved file is damaged. Timer records are removed on cancellation; older expired records are cleaned up when another timer starts. Clearing app storage removes app-private records and resets your saved progress.

Uninstalling does not remove files you exported elsewhere or copies held by another service. Remove those at the destination. We cannot remotely retrieve or delete records that remain only on your device.

Support correspondence is retained while needed to resolve your request, maintain necessary support records or meet legal obligations, then deleted when no longer needed. Provider-held data follows the relevant provider's retention rules. Contact us to request deletion of information you sent us.

Server access records. Installation identities, purchase verification and rewarded-access records are stored separately from your local cooking records. Subscription or reward expiry ends the corresponding access; it does not automatically delete the related verification or anti-replay records. The current service does not have a fixed automatic deletion schedule for these records, and uninstalling or clearing app storage does not itself send a server deletion request.

To request access to or deletion of server-held records, email the contact below. We may need non-secret information from your installation or Google Play transaction to locate and verify the request. Do not send an installation credential, bearer token, password or full payment card number. Requests need to account for records required to provide an existing entitlement, prevent transaction replay or meet applicable obligations. Deleting app data or requesting deletion from us does not cancel a Google Play subscription; manage cancellation in Google Play. Google and hosting-provider records are subject to their own retention practices.

7. Legal grounds and your rights

Where European or similar data-protection rules apply, requested app functions, including access verification and recipe or illustration delivery, are provided to perform our agreement with you. We rely on legitimate interests to respond to support requests and protect our services, and on legal obligations where applicable. Advertising-enabled builds request consent for advertising storage or processing where required before requesting ads.

Depending on your location and the processing involved, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to processing. You may withdraw consent where processing relies on it, without affecting earlier lawful processing. You may complain to your local data-protection authority. These rights have conditions and exceptions; see the European Data Protection Board's guidance.

To make a request, email the contact below. We may ask for proportionate information to verify that the request concerns your data. Do not send an identity document unless we explain why it is necessary. We do not make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about users.

8. Security, international processing and children

Local records use the app's private storage area; this is not a promise that all files are encrypted. Protect your device and exported files. No device or service can guarantee absolute security.

Advertising, website-hosting and support providers may process technical or support information in countries other than yours. Their policies explain applicable international-transfer arrangements and safeguards. Local saved cooking records are not automatically transferred by the current Android app.

The app is intended for adults and has no child accounts or child-specific data collection features. Do not include a child's personal information in support messages. If you believe such information has been provided to us, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate.
